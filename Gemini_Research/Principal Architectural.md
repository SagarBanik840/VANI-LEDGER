### Track 3: FinTech & Financial Inclusion

#### Project Codename & One-Line Pitch

**VANI-LEDGER:** A real-time vernacular voice-and-vision merchant reconciliation and UPI safety copilot that extracts structured ledger entries from handwritten bills via native VLMs, verifies counterparty risk in an isolated sandbox, and executes multi-party accounting via custom MCP tools.

#### The Real-World Bottleneck

Over 60 million Indian micro-merchants (kirana stores, rural wholesalers, and informal service providers) operate across fragmented payment channels—combining UPI QR voice-boxes, handwritten _bahi-khata_ credit slips, WhatsApp supplier invoices, and cash. Current FinTech apps fail these merchants for two reasons: first, legacy OCR pipelines mangle crumpled, multilingual Indian invoices containing mixed Hindi/English/regional scripts and handwritten math; second, merchants are increasingly targeted by social-engineering UPI collect-request scams and fake payment screenshots. Standard voice chatbots cannot deterministically reconcile a photographed paper invoice against live bank/UPI webhook ledgers or pause a fraudulent transfer before settlement.

#### End-to-End Execution Pipeline

A workflow begins when a merchant either snaps a photo of a handwritten supplier challan or speaks in Hinglish/vernacular into the `LiveKit` WebRTC audio stream: _"Ramesh Traders ka 4,200 rupaye ka bill check karo aur baaki credit clear kar do."_ Step 1 streams the audio frames through `LiveKit` to `Whisper-turbo` while simultaneously passing the invoice image through `Docling` and a Vision-Language Model (routed via EasyCLIProxy) to extract structured line items, GSTIN, handwritten totals, and bounding-box coordinates without legacy OCR. Step 2 passes the extracted structured JSON to the `PydanticAI` Reconciliation Agent, which queries PostgreSQL using hybrid `pgvector` phonetic embeddings plus BM25 lexical search to resolve "Ramesh Traders" against the merchant's historical ledger. Step 3 invokes the custom `FastMCP` Accounting & Risk Server: the `verify_invoice_math(version="2.0.0")` tool deterministically recomputes line-item quantities, discounts, and tax in Python, catching a 300-rupee arithmetic overcharge on the paper bill, while `simulate_upi_settlement()` checks the destination VPA against a sandboxed fraud-velocity graph (modeled on the 2026 hackathon winner _Scam Guard_). Step 4 renders the visual bounding box over the exact miscalculated line item on the merchant's screen and synthesizes a natural vernacular voice response via `Kokoro TTS` over `LiveKit` in under 400ms: warning the merchant of the 300-rupee discrepancy and staging an adjusted, cryptographically signed UPI intent payload awaiting biometric/PIN confirmation.

#### The 3-Minute Live Judge Demo Script

In the opening 30 seconds, the presenter places a real, crumpled, handwritten Hinglish wholesale invoice on camera and speaks to `VANI-LEDGER` in Hindi/English over a live `LiveKit` WebRTC stream. Within 1.5 seconds, the screen displays the invoice with interactive colored bounding boxes: green boxes over verified items and a pulsing red box highlighting a deliberate handwritten addition error (`₹4,200` written instead of the true sum `₹3,850`). At 1:00, the `FastMCP` inspector panel on the right shows the exact deterministic tool call (`reconcile_khata_entry`) executing an atomic SQL transaction preview in a sandboxed ledger while speaking back to the judge in crisp vernacular audio via `Kokoro TTS`. At 1:45, the presenter simulates a classic UPI collect-request scam (a spoofed supplier VPA requesting immediate settlement); the `React Flow` risk graph immediately pauses the transaction in a red escrow state, flags the VPA's velocity anomaly, and triggers an audible voice warning. In the final 30 seconds, the team switches EasyCLIProxy to local offline mode (`Whisper-turbo` + `Kokoro TTS` + local Ollama), proving that voice-to-SQL ledger queries execute with zero external internet dependency.
